  release 1).
- `02-ic2-complex-model.md` — the Imperial Conquest 2 model, recorded for later.

  the agenda they came from.
- `04-toy-app.md` — the toy testbed's design (parked).
- `06-testing-the-harness.md` — how a run is created and why runs do not share a
  repository.
- `08-geoclick2027-harness.md` — the Geoclick2027 model, two harnesses in one
  repository.

Two documents were removed in the descope: the competition design (`05`) — the
`merge: auto` policy in the harness is what remains of it — and the
feature-request guide (`07`), whose content lives in `ROADMAP.md` itself.
